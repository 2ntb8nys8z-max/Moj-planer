# Dated Yaga guidance

The original workbook is read-only. Its SHA-256 is `e8051e845ed880e37b4d1404acbcdba1ff79024872a3b6a2240dcd41990e8cae`. The existing full-text import is retained unchanged. `scripts/import-yaga-guidance.py WORKBOOK.xlsx` validates the dated audit excerpts and imports source row metadata, chapter rows, categories and legend into `brief-guidance-data.js`. Audit selections are in `data/yaga-editorial-selections.json`; H and P/S candidates are review holds, not approved advice.

`brief-guidance.js` processes every dated row using the same rules. It separates action clauses from transit descriptions and stories; records file/hash/sheet/cell/exact Unicode spans; preserves negations, conditions and verbal periods; resolves explicit dated source references; and holds conflicting, dependent, ritual, herbal and sensitive financial candidates. Approved here means eligible under these source extraction rules, not human or medical approval. Raw source material, chapter subtitles/quotes and both sides of each conflict remain in the expansion. The original time differences are retained as diagnostics, without manufactured corrections.

Time validity distinguishes numeric windows, unknown starts, verbal ends and explicit whole days. Missing hours do not become an explicit all-day restriction. Verbal transit periods are retained without invented calendar end dates. Advice is never propagated to neighboring dates. Primary excerpts and explanatory context are distinct evidence entries.

The narrator receives only eligible guidance and filtered event explanations. It receives neither review-held excerpts nor raw herbal practices. Optional spoken tips are complete source units, with attribution: the voice budget may drop an entire tip but cannot trim away its prohibition, condition or time window. Weather, calendar, route and departure requirements retain priority. Revalidating the result is idempotent.

`yaga-review.html` provides a dated editorial queue, source evidence, conflicts and a JSON export. Review includes contract encouragement versus a full-day restriction (11 December 2026), berries straight from the bush versus washing (3 July 2027), the resolved nettle warning reference (16 March to 1 March 2027), herbal/medication warnings, symbolic practices, ambiguous pronouns and source time differences. These are visible source records, not instructions to the app or personalized health/legal/financial advice.

Verification: all 426 contiguous dates; exact evidence spans; 202 void dates (113 numeric windows, 59 verbal night endings, 23 unknown starts, 7 explicit whole days); 80 message-check tips; 16 chapters; eight report examples; source-held excerpts; original XLSX hash; main/expanded rendering; voice completeness and budget; opt-out and standard calendar independence. Mobile CI adds Chromium and WebKit at widths 320, 390 and 430. Its providers, GPS and writer are fixtures, not a live AI/provider quality assessment.
